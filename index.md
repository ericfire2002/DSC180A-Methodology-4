## Quarter 2 Project Brainstorming

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

What I found most interesting this quarter was how to use LLM’s to automatically generate high quality educational content. I am interested in generating questions with LLM that fits the content of a class and a specified Bloom’s taxonomy level. This could be useful for instructors to create good practice questions for students while also saving time on writing it.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to examine whether different prompting strategies for the LLM yield better or worse quality questions. In particular, I would test whether the amount or relevance of context would influence the quality of questions generated. I would evaluate the questions on correctness, variety, contextual fit, answerability, and Bloom’s Taxonomy fit.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One change I might make is to experiment with different ways of providing content to the LLM. Instead of giving more context to the LLM, I would experiment with giving more specific content that is more relevant to the topic of the question being generated. This could produce more accurate and higher quality questions by reducing irrelevant information. 

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I am interested in using RAG (retrieval-augmented questions), different prompting strategies, and automated evaluation for generated questions. RAG can help automatically detect relevant course materials, whereas trying different prompting strategies can help improve the structure and quality of questions. Also, I would like to compare LLM-based evaluation vs human review when assessing generated questions.
